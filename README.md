# Pinplank: Screw Puzzle

A free screw pin puzzle for Android: unscrew the pins that have no plank on top, drop each screw into the box of its colour, and watch the wooden planks fall away. Every level is dealt so it can be solved, and you only lose when no free screw fits a box.

## How it plays

- **Tap free screws.** A screw with no plank on top of it can come out.
- **Fill the colour boxes.** Each screw flies into the open box of its colour. Three fill a box and the next one arrives.
- **Holding holes.** A screw with no box yet waits in one of five holes and jumps in by itself when its box opens.
- **Drop the planks.** A plank with no screws left falls off the board. Clear every screw to win.
- **No forced fail.** Full holes only block screws that don't fit. The level ends only when no free screw fits any open box, and even then you can add a hole.
- **Always solvable.** Every board is dealt along an order that clears it.
- **Colour-blind friendly.** Every screw head carries its own shape mark.
- **Daily Board and check-in.** A daily board for everyone, plus a 7-day check-in for coins.
- **Plays offline.** No Wi-Fi, no account. Light and dark themes.

Free, no purchases. Supported by optional videos and an occasional ad between levels, never during one.
